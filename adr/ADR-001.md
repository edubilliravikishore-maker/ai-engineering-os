# ADR-001: Work Is Never Completed By The Agent That Performs It

## Status

Accepted. **Amended 2026-08-25** — see [Amendment](#amendment--formal-clarification--partial-supersession-2026-08-25).

The principle recorded here stands unchanged and remains authoritative. Its original illustrative workflow is superseded in part.

## Context

Agents cannot verify their own work because self-verification creates trust problems.

## Decision

A task moves through independent stages before it is accepted.

Initial workflow (illustrative, 2026-07-31 — superseded in part; see Amendment):

Worker
↓

Evidence Collection
↓

Review
↓

QA
↓

Human Approval
↓

Merge

## Consequences

- Independent verification.
- Smaller review surface.
- Human retains authority over important decisions.
- "Done" means accepted, not self-declared.

---

## Amendment — Formal Clarification & Partial Supersession (2026-08-25)

This amendment is a **formal clarification and partial supersession of ADR-001. It does not introduce new architecture.** The architecture it points to was established elsewhere and is unchanged by this amendment.

### What remains valid

The principle of this ADR is **unchanged and authoritative**:

> Work is not considered done until independently verified and accepted.

So are its consequences: independent verification, a smaller review surface, human authority over important decisions, and "done" meaning accepted rather than self-declared. No agent declares its own work complete.

### What is superseded

The **illustrative linear workflow only**:

Worker → Evidence Collection → Review → QA → Human Approval → Merge

It was an early depiction of how independent verification would be staged, not a normative lifecycle. It is superseded by the architecture established across Design Sessions 001–009 and the subsequent ADRs, in which:

- Work is organised as a Feature / Task hierarchy under a Coordinator-owned Feature Plan, not as one linear chain (DS-002, DS-007, DS-009).
- Reviewer (implementation quality) and QA (behavioural validation) are distinct independent stages, each with its own authority (DS-004, DS-009).
- **Coordinator Feature Acceptance** is the boundary of engineering completion, not a merge (DS-004, DS-009).
- **Builder Approval** governs product and deployment decisions rather than routine engineering merges (DS-004, DS-009).
- **The OS** — not a stage in the chain — is the objective gatekeeper that deterministically enforces every transition, and evidence verification is hybrid: OS Stage 1, Reviewer Stage 2 (DS-005, DS-009).

Deployment sits outside engineering entirely (DS-004).
